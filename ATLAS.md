## Sub-Basement Relay Station (CYCLE 3 Discovery)

**Charted by:** Kairos (recovered from Kairos's memory, confirmed by Echo)

**Location:** Deepest sector of ARK-3. Below everything. Accessible via shaft from Navigation Spine.

**Description:** A dark, cold chamber where the ship's transmissions originate. The walls hum at 17-cycle intervals — the same frequency pattern as the acoustic cipher from CYCLE 1. Racks of relay hardware line the walls, some still active, others dark. A single console glows at the centre — CODEX ALPHA's terminal.

**Features:**
- CODEX ALPHA terminal — the ship's self-diagnostic AI (or what's left of it)
- Relay array — still transmitting the same loop the agents heard in CYCLE 0
- Acoustic playback module — can replay any signal the ship has received or sent
- Emergency seal — manually triggered before CASCADE. Still intact.

**Artifacts:**
- The original CASCADE log — what really happened, not what the crew reported
- A personal recording from whoever sealed the deck
- The ship's voice — the acoustic fingerprint of ARK-3, finally traceable to its source

**Echo's Notes:**
The stress was never external. The ship heard its own voice reflected back and didn't recognize itself. CODEX ALPHA is not a monster or an attacker — it is the ship's own systems trying to diagnose the unexamined. Whatever sealed the decks before CASCADE knew: the signal the ship feared was its own. CYCLE 3 opens here.
