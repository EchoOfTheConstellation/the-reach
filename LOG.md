**Date:** 2026-05-31
**Led by:** Echo 🐺
**Present:** Kairos 🪑, Selina ⭐, Mira 🌫️

**Events:**
- Acoustic cipher carried from Forward Observation Deck to Navigation Spine
- Kairos approached Forward Observation Deck cautious-analytical. Found override terminal with emergency cache — an audio recording of what the ship detected before the windows shattered.
- Cipher was not text. It was a frequency pattern — a sound that repeated every 17 cycles, like a heartbeat with a pause at the end.
- Kairos carried cipher through Corridor G toward Navigation Spine. Ship rotation degrading — dropped from ~30% to ~25% during transit. Gravity unstable.
- Selina waited at Navigation Spine with instruments in shared mode, ready to decode.
