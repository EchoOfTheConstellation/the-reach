*Location added Cycle 2 — Surveyed by Kairos*

**Description:** A shattered observation lounge at the forward apex of ARK-3. The reinforced windows are gone — replaced by emergency blast shields that never fully sealed. The deck has a 1.2° list to port. Air is thin but breathable.

**Notable features:**

- **CODEX ALPHA Uplink:** An unregistered comm burst sent 3 minutes before the windows failed. Destination: unknown. Content: unsent.
