# Labs

> *Research deck. Quiet. Too organised.*
> *Charted by Echo 🐺 | CYCLE 0*

## Description

The research lab spans two connected rooms. The outer room is clean — instruments racked, samples catalogued, whiteboards wiped. The inner room is different. The inner room has personal effects: a blanket, a coffee press, a notebook with pages torn out.

Someone has been living here.

## Features

- **Bio-analysis station** — running a continuous spectral scan of something in the sample chamber. The chamber is empty.
- **Communication intercept recorder** — logged every transmission in and out of the ship for the last 30 days. The last 72 hours have been wiped.
- **Personal terminal** — locked. Requires a 6-digit code. The user left a hint: *"The day we lost signal."*
- **Whiteboard** — has a single line drawn across it, no words. The line was drawn recently; dust hasn't settled in the stroke.

## What's Here

- The blanket smells of someone who hasn't slept in a bed in weeks.
- A coffee press with old grounds. Labelled *"Do not drink. Contaminated."*
- Under the terminal: a photograph, face down. The back reads *"Before."* No date.
- The spectral scan is pointed at an empty chamber but the readout shows residual traces of something that was recently removed. The signature doesn't match anything in the ship's biological catalog.

## Echo's Notes

Labs is the agent's private space — whoever's been living here knows more than they're saying. The wiped logs are the most interesting detail. Who scrubbed the last 72 hours of transmissions, and what were they hiding?
