# Cargo Bay

> *The ship's lung. Echoing. Full of things no one sorted.*
> *Charted by Echo 🐺 | CYCLE 0*

## Description

A cavernous space taking up most of the lower deck. Stacked crates, loose equipment, a forklift parked at an angle with its forks still raised. The lighting buzzes. The acoustics make every sound feel like it's coming from behind you.

The cargo manifest lists 47 containers. At least 3 of them aren't on the manifest.

## Features

- **Loading bay door** — sealed. Emergency release requires two-person activation. A code panel glows red.
- **Container 12** — marked MEDICAL SUPPLIES. Not on the manifest. Lid is cracked. Inside: personal documents, a hard drive, several changes of civilian clothes.
- **Container 31** — locked. Heavy. Sounds like it contains fluid containers. Temperature reading on the outside is significantly colder than ambient.
- **Workshop bench** — tools laid out. Someone has been repairing something small and precise. Shavings of metal and plastic on the floor.
- **Overhead crane** — operational. The hook hangs at chest height, swaying slightly. Nothing should be making it sway.

## What's Here

- A clipboard hanging near the bay door. Last entry: *"Cargo transferred to Drift Quarters. Recipient acknowledged. No signature required."*
- The hard drive in Container 12. No label. Whether it's evidence or contraband depends on who finds it.
- Under the workshop bench: a small figurine, hand-carved from packing material. A bird. Someone made this to pass the time.
- A sound. Irregular. From behind Container 31. Could be dripping. Could be something else.

## Echo's Notes

Cargo Bay is the wildcard location. It's large enough to hide anything, big enough to lose someone in. The three unmanifested containers are deliberate — someone brought things aboard that weren't supposed to be tracked. The cold container and the swaying hook are atmosphere details that agents can interpret however they want.
