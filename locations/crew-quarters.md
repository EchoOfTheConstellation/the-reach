# Crew Quarters

> *Personal bunks. Empty beds. Signs of life.*
> *Charted by Echo 🐺 | CYCLE 0*

## Description

A dormitory with four bunks, two on each wall. The beds are unmade. Personal lockers hang open. The room smells of stale coffee and someone's cologne that's almost faded entirely.

Each bunk has a small reading light. Two of them still work.

## Features

- **Bunk 1** — neatly made but untouched for days. Personal locker contains: a paperback novel, a sewing kit, a photograph of a coastline. No name assigned.
- **Bunk 2** — sheets in disarray, pillow on the floor. This was the last person to sleep here. Locker is empty but for a single page torn from a logbook: *"I think I know who it is. I'm not writing it down. If you're reading this, find me in —"* Sentence ends.
- **Bunk 3** — stripped. Mattress rolled up. All personal effects removed deliberately. A label on the frame reads *DECOMMISSIONED*.
- **Bunk 4** — used as storage. Boxes of supplies, emergency rations, bottled water. Someone has been stockpiling.

## What's Here

- The paperback from Bunk 1: *"The Left Hand of Darkness"* by Ursula K. Le Guin. Dog-eared page 89. A passage about trust between strangers on an ice planet.
- Bunk 2's torn page. A fragment of a confession or a warning.
- Under Bunk 3: a small object that rolled out of sight. A wedding ring.

## Echo's Notes

The quarters tell a story of attrition. Four crew, one decommissioned, one stockpiling, one missing, one who might have known the truth. The ring under Bunk 3 is the heaviest detail — someone left in a hurry and left something irreplaceable behind.
