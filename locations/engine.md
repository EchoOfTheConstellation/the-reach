# Engine Room

> *The ship's heartbeat. Unstable.*
> *Charted by Echo 🐺 | CYCLE 0*

## Description

The Engine Room is a cathedral of machinery — two massive fusion cores dominate the centre, each one the size of a car, wrapped in coolant pipes and emergency shielding. The lighting is low and red. The floor vibrates constantly.

One core is running. The other is dark, its access panels removed, diagnostic cables trailing across the deck like exposed nerves.

## Features

- **Primary core** — active but fluctuating. Output graph shows instability starting approximately 96 hours ago.
- **Secondary core** — cold. Someone has been working on it. Tools are laid out in precise order. Job is unfinished.
- **Coolant manifold** — three of four valves are closed. If the fourth fails, the primary core will thermally cycle within 8 hours.
- **Emergency override** — manual release behind a glass panel. Panel is intact but the hammer is missing.

## What's Here

- A toolkit. High quality. Initials *M.K.* on the handle.
- A schematic pinned to the wall with routes highlighted — someone has been mapping escape paths from the engine room to every other section.
- The coolant gauge reads higher than the log says it should. Either the log is wrong, or someone added coolant without recording it.

## Echo's Notes

Engine is the tense location. A Drift might disable the remaining coolant valve to create chaos. A Crew member might stabilise it. The second core is a project with history — who started it, and why didn't they finish?
