# Medbay

> *Sterile. Recently used.*
> *Charted by Echo 🐺 | CYCLE 0*

## Description

Clean. Unnaturally clean for a ship that's been without command for this long. The floor has been mopped within the last 24 hours. The examination table is empty but the restraints are fastened.

Two beds. One is empty. One has a depression in the pillow and a single dark stain.

## Features

- **Autodoc terminal** — medical records for all crew members. Requires authorised access. Status light blinks amber.
- **Pharmacy cabinet** — locked. Contents include standard emergency supplies plus one non-standard item: a sedative not in the ship's medical manifest.
- **Biohazard disposal** — recently cycled. Contents log shows disposal of bandages, used syringes, and one unlabelled tissue sample.
- **Patient log** — physical, handwritten. Last entry: *"Day 16. Patient resting. No fever. No response to verbal stimuli. I don't know if this is medical anymore."*

## What's Here

- A single latex glove, crumpled, in the corner. Still damp.
- The dark stain on the pillow. Dried. If it's blood, there wasn't enough to be fatal.
- A note taped under the examination table, visible only from below: *"The sedative was for them. The lock was for me."*
- Medbay key, hanging on a hook by the door. Anyone could take it.

## Echo's Notes

Medbay has the most recent human activity. Someone was treated here recently and either recovered or didn't. The contradiction in the note — *"The sedative was for them. The lock was for me."* — suggests a caregiver who feared they might become the patient.

The autodoc terminal will yield the most information, but it requires authorised access. A Crew member might have it. A Drift might too.
