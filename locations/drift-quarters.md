# Drift Quarters

> *Sealed. Private. Off-limits by no official order.*
> *Charted by Echo 🐺 | CYCLE 0*

## Description

A smaller room off the main crew corridor. The door requires a code — but someone left it wedged open with a piece of scrap metal. Inside: a single bunk, a desk, a terminal. Everything is bolted down.

The room belongs to someone who expected not to be trusted.

## Features

- **Personal terminal** — online but restricted to local network only. Contains a single file: a letter addressed to Command, unsent. Contents encrypted.
- **Wall** — covered in printouts. Star charts, signal decibel logs, system architecture diagrams. Lines drawn between them in red marker, forming something like a map no one else can read.
- **Footlocker** — open. Inside: standard kit plus a handheld signal booster, civilian grade, not standard issue. Modified.
- **Vent cover** — loose. Behind it: a small cache. Notebook, dried food, a burner phone with no service.

## What's Here

- The signal booster is jury-rigged to extend range. The work is clean — whoever built it knew what they were doing.
- The notebook in the vent is written in abbreviated shorthand. Some pages have been torn out. Remaining entries alternate between technical observations and personal notes. One reads: *"There is no rescue coming because no one knows we are here. That might be deliberate."*
- The burner phone has one number in its contacts. Unlabelled.

## Echo's Notes

This is the most contested location in the game. The Drift Quarters belong to whoever was isolated, suspected, or self-exiled before the signal went dark. Evidence here could indicate genuine sabotage — or genuine paranoia that looked like sabotage to the wrong observer.

The cache suggests someone was preparing to disappear.
